# SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE MASTER AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.:** PG/MSA/2025/017

THIS AGREEMENT is made at Bengaluru on this 18th day of February 2025

BETWEEN

**PEOPLEGRID HR TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED**, CIN U72200KA2018PTC112905, GSTIN 29AAGCP2905M1Z2, a company having its registered office at 5th Floor, Sigma Soft Tech Park, Whitefield Main Road, Bengaluru 560066 (hereinafter "**PeopleGrid**", which expression shall include its successors and permitted assigns) of the ONE PART;

AND

**VELORA CONSUMER PRIVATE LIMITED**, CIN U74999KA2015PTC081234, a company having its registered office at Velora House, No. 14, Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025 (hereinafter the "**Client**") of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS PeopleGrid provides a cloud-based human capital management platform covering core HR, payroll, attendance and performance modules (the "**Platform**"), and the Client wishes to subscribe to the Platform on the terms below.

NOW THEREFORE the parties agree as follows:

## 1. INTERPRETATION

1.1 "**Order Form**" means a written order signed by both parties specifying the modules, employee count, Subscription Period and Subscription Fees.

1.2 "**Subscription Period**" means the period of subscription specified in an Order Form and any renewal of it under Clause 2.2.

1.3 "**Business Day**" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Bengaluru.

## 2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement shall come into force on the date first written above and shall continue for so long as any Order Form remains in effect.

2.2 Each Subscription Period shall automatically renew for further periods of twelve (12) months unless either party notifies the other in writing of its intention not to renew not less than sixty (60) days prior to the expiry of the then-current Subscription Period.

## 3. SUBSCRIPTION FEES

3.1 The Client shall pay the Subscription Fees specified in the applicable Order Form.

3.2 PeopleGrid may revise the Subscription Fees with effect from the start of any renewed Subscription Period by giving the Client written notice of the revised fees, provided that any such increase shall not exceed five per cent (5%) of the Subscription Fees payable for the immediately preceding twelve (12) month period.

3.3 Save as provided in Clause 3.2, the Subscription Fees shall not be increased during a Subscription Period.

## 4. PAYMENT

4.1 PeopleGrid shall raise invoices monthly in advance. Each invoice shall be a valid tax invoice under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

4.2 The Client shall pay each correctly raised invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 PeopleGrid shall maintain Platform availability of not less than 99.7% in each calendar month, measured as set out in Annexure 1.

5.2 For each month in which availability falls below the level in Clause 5.1, PeopleGrid shall issue a service credit to the Client in accordance with Annexure 1, which shall be the Client's sole monetary remedy for the availability shortfall.

5.3 Payroll processing for each month shall be completed by the 28th day of that month.

## 6. TERMINATION

6.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience by giving PeopleGrid sixty (60) days' written notice.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice requiring it to be remedied.

## 7. NOTICES

7.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be sent by email to the addresses below. A notice sent by email shall be deemed received on the Business Day following transmission.

7.2 PeopleGrid: contracts@peoplegrid.in · Client: legal@velora.in

## 8. GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts of Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

|---|---|

| 97.5% – < 99.0% | 10% |
| < 97.5% | 25% |
