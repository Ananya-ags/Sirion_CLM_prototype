## 2. SUBSCRIPTION AND SERVICES

2.1 PeopleGrid shall provide the Client and its Affiliates with access to the Platform for the number of Active Employees stated in each Order Form, during each Subscription Period, in accordance with this Agreement.

2.2 PeopleGrid shall host the Platform in data centres located in India and shall not move the production environment to another hosting provider without giving the Client sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

2.3 PeopleGrid shall process the Client's monthly payroll on the Platform, generate payslips, bank transfer files and statutory returns, and make them available to the Client's payroll team for approval before release.

2.4 PeopleGrid shall provide first-line support to the Client's HR administrators through its support portal and telephone line between 08:00 and 20:00 IST on Business Days, and on a 24x7 basis during the five (5) days before each payroll cut-off.

2.5 PeopleGrid may update the Platform from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the functionality of the modules subscribed to by the Client.

## 3. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 The Client shall provide complete and accurate Payroll Inputs for each month by the 20th day of that month, through the Platform's input templates or an agreed integration.

3.2 The Client shall be responsible for the accuracy of employee master data entered by its users, including bank account details, tax regime elections and statutory identifiers such as PAN, UAN and ESIC numbers.

3.3 The Client shall nominate not more than ten (10) super-administrators and shall ensure that user access is removed when an administrator leaves the Client's employment.

3.4 Where the Client provides Payroll Inputs after the date in Clause 3.1, PeopleGrid shall use reasonable endeavours to complete the payroll run on time but shall not be liable for any resulting delay.

## 4. GOVERNANCE AND CHANGE CONTROL

4.1 Each Party shall nominate a relationship manager who shall be the primary point of contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Either Party may replace its relationship manager by written notice to the other.

4.2 The relationship managers shall meet monthly to review performance, open issues, risks and planned changes, and PeopleGrid shall circulate minutes within five (5) Business Days of each meeting.

4.3 The Parties shall hold a quarterly business review attended by senior representatives of both Parties to review the overall relationship, performance trends, improvement initiatives and the forward plan.

4.4 Either Party may propose a change to the Services by submitting a Change Request. PeopleGrid shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a Change Request from the Client, provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on scope, timelines, resources and Subscription Fees.

4.5 No Change Request shall be binding unless it is signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. Until a Change Request is signed, the Parties shall continue to perform this Agreement without regard to the proposed change.

4.6 PeopleGrid shall not be entitled to any additional Subscription Fees for work performed outside the agreed scope unless that work was the subject of a signed Change Request.

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Recipient shall: (a) keep the Discloser's Confidential Information confidential; (b) use it only for the purpose of performing or receiving the benefit of this Agreement; and (c) not disclose it to any person except as permitted by this Clause 5.

5.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Personnel, Affiliates, auditors and professional advisers who need to know it for the purposes of this Agreement, provided that the Recipient ensures that each such person is bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than those in this Clause.

5.3 The obligations in this Clause do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully in the Recipient's possession before disclosure; (c) is received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

5.4 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Applicable Law or by order of a court or Governmental Authority, provided that, where lawful, it gives the Discloser prompt notice and cooperates with any attempt by the Discloser to obtain a protective order.

5.5 On the Discloser's written request, and in any event on termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, save that the Recipient may retain copies required by Applicable Law or held in routine back-up systems, which shall remain subject to this Clause.

5.6 The obligations in this Clause survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years, and indefinitely in respect of trade secrets and Personal Data.

5.7 Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach of this Clause and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in respect of any threatened or actual breach.

## 6. DATA PROTECTION

6.1 The Parties acknowledge that, in respect of Personal Data processed under this Agreement, the Client is the Data Fiduciary and PeopleGrid is a Data Processor acting on behalf of the Client.

6.2 PeopleGrid shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Client, only for the purposes of this Agreement and in compliance with Data Protection Law. PeopleGrid shall promptly inform the Client if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

6.3 PeopleGrid shall implement and maintain reasonable security safeguards, including the measures agreed between the Parties in writing, to protect Personal Data against personal data breach, as required by Section 8(5) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

6.4 PeopleGrid shall notify the Client of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it, and shall provide all information and assistance the Client reasonably requires to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals.

6.5 PeopleGrid shall assist the Client in responding to requests from Data Principals to exercise their rights under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including rights of access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal.

6.6 PeopleGrid shall not transfer Personal Data outside India except to a country or territory that has not been restricted by notification of the Central Government under Section 16 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and only with the Client's prior written consent.

6.7 PeopleGrid shall ensure that its Personnel who have access to Personal Data are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and receive training in data protection at least once a year.

6.8 On completion of the purpose for which Personal Data was provided, or on the Client's request, PeopleGrid shall erase that Personal Data and certify the erasure in writing, save where retention is required by Applicable Law.

## 7. INFORMATION SECURITY

7.1 PeopleGrid shall maintain an information security programme in accordance with Good Industry Practice and aligned to ISO/IEC 27001 or an equivalent standard, and shall comply with the information security requirements that the other Party notifies to it in writing.

7.2 PeopleGrid shall comply with the directions issued by CERT-In under Section 70B(6) of the Information Technology Act, 2000, including the requirement to report notified cyber incidents within six (6) hours of noticing them.

7.3 PeopleGrid shall ensure that access to the Client's systems and Customer Data is limited to named Personnel, protected by multi-factor authentication and reviewed at least quarterly, and that access is revoked within one (1) Business Day of a person ceasing to require it.

7.4 PeopleGrid shall commission vulnerability assessments at least twice a year and an independent penetration test at least once a year, and shall remediate critical findings within fifteen (15) days and high findings within thirty (30) days of identification.

7.5 PeopleGrid shall maintain logs of access to Customer Data for at least one hundred and eighty (180) days and make them available to the Client on request.

## 8. STATUTORY AND PAYROLL COMPLIANCE

8.1 PeopleGrid shall configure and maintain on the Platform the statutory rules applicable to the Client's payroll, including provident fund, employees' state insurance, professional tax, labour welfare fund and tax deducted at source under the Income-tax Act, 1961.

8.2 PeopleGrid shall implement any change in statutory rates, ceilings or formats within thirty (30) days after the change is notified by the relevant Governmental Authority, or by the effective date of the change if later.

8.3 PeopleGrid shall generate Form 16, Form 24Q and the electronic challan-cum-returns for provident fund in the formats prescribed from time to time, for review and filing by the Client.

8.4 The Client remains responsible for the filing of statutory returns and the payment of statutory dues, and PeopleGrid's obligation is limited to the accurate computation and preparation of the relevant files.

## 9. INDEMNITIES

9.1 PeopleGrid shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Client, its Affiliates and their respective officers and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or in connection with: (a) any claim that the Services or any materials delivered by PeopleGrid infringe the Intellectual Property Rights of a third party; (b) any breach by PeopleGrid of Clause 5 or Clause 6; (c) any breach by PeopleGrid of Applicable Law, including labour and tax laws; and (d) death, personal injury or damage to tangible property caused by the negligence of PeopleGrid or its Personnel.

9.2 The Client shall indemnify PeopleGrid from and against all Losses arising out of any claim that Customer Data or materials supplied by the Client infringe the Intellectual Property Rights of a third party.

9.3 A Party seeking indemnity shall: (a) notify the indemnifying Party of the claim promptly; (b) allow the indemnifying Party to conduct the defence and settlement of the claim; (c) provide reasonable assistance at the indemnifying Party's cost; and (d) not admit liability or settle the claim without the indemnifying Party's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

9.4 If any part of the Services becomes, or in PeopleGrid's reasonable opinion is likely to become, the subject of an infringement claim, PeopleGrid shall at its own cost and option either procure the right for the Client to continue using it or modify or replace it so that it is non-infringing without material loss of functionality.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either Party's liability for: (a) death or personal injury caused by its negligence; (b) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (c) breach of Clause 5 or Clause 6; (d) its indemnity obligations under Clause 9; or (e) any liability that cannot be limited or excluded under Applicable Law.

10.2 Subject to Clause 10.1, neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, special or consequential loss, or for any loss of profits, revenue, goodwill or anticipated savings, whether arising in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise.

10.3 Subject to Clause 10.1, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement in any contract year shall not exceed an amount equal to one and a half times of the Subscription Fees paid and payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

10.4 Service credits or fee credits, where provided for in this Agreement, are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are not an estimate of the loss that may be suffered; they are without prejudice to any other right or remedy of the Client.

## 11. TERM

11.1 This Agreement shall come into force on the date first written above and shall continue for so long as any Order Form remains in effect.

11.2 Each Subscription Period shall automatically renew for further periods of twelve (12) months unless either party notifies the other in writing of its intention not to renew not less than the Non-Renewal Notice Period prior to the expiry of the then-current Subscription Period.

## 12. SUBSCRIPTION FEES

12.1 The Client shall pay the Subscription Fees specified in the applicable Order Form.

12.2 PeopleGrid may revise the Subscription Fees with effect from the start of any renewed Subscription Period by giving the Client written notice of the revised fees, provided that any such increase shall not exceed five per cent (5%) of the Subscription Fees payable for the immediately preceding twelve (12) month period.

12.3 Save as provided in Clause 12.2, the Subscription Fees shall not be increased during a Subscription Period.

12.4 Active Employees above the count licensed in an Order Form shall be charged at the per-employee rate stated in that Order Form, calculated on the highest number of Active Employees in each calendar quarter.

## 13. PAYMENT

13.1 PeopleGrid shall raise invoices monthly in advance. Each invoice shall be a valid tax invoice under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017.

13.2 The Client shall pay each correctly raised invoice within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

13.3 If the Client disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify PeopleGrid within fifteen (15) days of receipt and pay the undisputed portion in accordance with Clause 13.2.

13.4 The Client may deduct tax at source under the Income-tax Act, 1961 and shall issue the corresponding certificates within the period prescribed by that Act.

## 14. SERVICE LEVELS

14.1 PeopleGrid shall maintain Platform availability of not less than 99.7% in each calendar month, measured as set out in Annexure 1.

14.2 For each month in which availability falls below the level in Clause 14.1, PeopleGrid shall issue a service credit to the Client in accordance with Annexure 1, which shall be the Client's sole monetary remedy for the availability shortfall.

14.3 Payroll processing for each month shall be completed by the 28th day of that month.

14.4 PeopleGrid shall send the Client a service report by the seventh (7th) Business Day of each month, showing Availability, payroll completion dates and support tickets for the previous month.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience by giving PeopleGrid sixty (60) days' written notice.

15.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach which remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice requiring it to be remedied.

15.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party is admitted into a corporate insolvency resolution process or liquidation under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.

## 16. CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

16.1 For ninety (90) days after termination or expiry, PeopleGrid shall allow the Client to export all employee, payroll and attendance records in CSV and PDF formats.

16.2 PeopleGrid shall provide year-to-date payroll registers and statutory summaries for the financial year in which termination takes effect, so that the Client can complete its annual filings.

16.3 After the export period, PeopleGrid shall delete the Client's data from its production and back-up systems and certify the deletion in writing on request.

## 17. FORCE MAJEURE

17.1 A "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party, including acts of God, flood, earthquake, epidemic or pandemic, war, terrorism, riot, civil commotion, strikes (other than strikes of the affected Party's own Personnel), lockouts, fire, explosion, and acts of any Governmental Authority, but excluding lack of funds.

17.2 Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations to the extent caused by a Force Majeure Event, provided that the affected Party: (a) notifies the other Party as soon as reasonably practicable of the nature and expected duration of the Force Majeure Event; and (b) uses all reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effect.

17.3 PeopleGrid shall maintain and test, at least once a year, a business continuity and disaster recovery plan, and a Force Majeure Event shall not excuse PeopleGrid from performing that plan.

17.4 If a Force Majeure Event prevents the performance of all or a material part of the Services for a continuous period of more than forty-five (45) days, the unaffected Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice.
