# ANNEXURE B — RATES AND FUEL INDEX

to the Logistics Services Master Agreement dated 11 March 2025 between SwiftMove Logistics Private Limited and Velora Consumer Private Limited.

## B1. FIXED CHARGES

| Item | Monthly charge (₹, excl. GST) |
|---|---|
| Dedicated fleet — 14 LCVs with drivers, 26 operating days | 3,05,000 |
| Hub handling & sortation (Hoskote hub) | 36,667 |
| **Total base monthly fixed charge** | **3,41,667** |

## B2. FUEL INDEX MECHANISM

B2.1 Base diesel price: ₹88.50 per litre (retail selling price, Bengaluru, as published by Indian Oil Corporation on 1 March 2025).

B2.2 For every ₹1.00 per litre increase or decrease in the average Bengaluru diesel price for the billing month compared to the base price, the Dedicated fleet charge shall be increased or decreased by 0.4%.

B2.3 The fuel adjustment shall be shown as a separate line item on each invoice.

## B3. VARIABLE CHARGES

B3.1 Ad-hoc vehicles beyond the dedicated fleet: ₹4,200 per vehicle-day, only against a written requisition from the Customer's logistics manager.
