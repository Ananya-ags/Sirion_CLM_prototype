2. PERIOD OF AGREEMENT

2.1 This Agreement shall be in force from 01.01.2024 to 31.12.2025.

2.2 Upon expiry of the period stated in Clause 2.1, unless terminated earlier, this Agreement shall continue on a month-to-month basis on the same terms and conditions until terminated by either party by giving not less than thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.

3. CHARGES

3.1 The Company shall pay the Service Provider a sum of Rs. 22,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty Two Lakhs only) per annum, payable in equal monthly instalments of Rs. 1,83,333/-, exclusive of GST.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 The Service Provider shall submit its invoice in the first week of each month along with proof of payment of wages, PF and ESI for the previous month. The Company shall release payment within forty-five (45) days from receipt of a correct invoice.
