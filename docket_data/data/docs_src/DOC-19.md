## 1. SERVICES

1.1 Stratus shall provide managed Kubernetes hosting, object storage and content-delivery services as specified in each Order Schedule (the "**Cloud Services**"). Each Order Schedule forms part of this Agreement.

## 2. SUBSCRIPTION TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 The initial subscription term commences on 1 May 2025 and ends on 30 April 2027 (the "**Initial Subscription Term**").

2.2 Thereafter, the subscription shall automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

## 3. FEES

3.1 Fees are stated and payable in United States Dollars (USD) as set out in the applicable Order Schedule.

3.2 Stratus may, effective from the start of any renewal term, increase the Fees by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index (All Items) published by the Singapore Department of Statistics over the preceding twelve months, provided that any such increase shall not exceed three per cent (3%).

3.3 Fees are exclusive of all taxes. Where Customer is required to account for Indian GST on the Cloud Services under the reverse-charge mechanism, Customer shall do so directly and Stratus shall not charge GST.

## 4. PAYMENT

4.1 Stratus shall invoice monthly in advance. Customer shall pay each invoice by wire transfer within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date. Bank charges in India are borne by Customer; correspondent bank charges are borne by Stratus.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 Stratus shall provide the Cloud Services with a monthly availability of 99.95% for the managed Kubernetes control plane.

5.2 If Stratus fails to meet the availability in Clause 5.1, Customer shall be entitled to service credits as set out in Schedule A (Service Level Agreement).

## 6. TERMINATION

6.1 Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. Prepaid Fees for the then-current month are non-refundable.

6.2 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

## 7. NOTICES

7.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent by email to legal@stratuscloud.sg (for Stratus) and legal@velora.in (for Customer).

## 8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Singapore.

8.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the SIAC for the time being in force. The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore.
