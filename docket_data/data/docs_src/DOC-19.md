## 2. SERVICES

2.1 Stratus shall provide managed Kubernetes hosting, object storage and content-delivery services as specified in each Order Schedule (the "**Cloud Services**"). Each Order Schedule forms part of this Agreement.

2.2 Unless an Order Schedule states otherwise, Stratus shall provide the Cloud Services from the Mumbai Region, and shall not move Customer's production workloads to another Region without Customer's prior written consent.

2.3 Stratus shall patch the managed control plane, node operating systems and platform components within the windows described in Schedule A, and shall give Customer at least five (5) Business Days' notice of any change that requires action by Customer.

2.4 Customer remains responsible for its own applications, container images, configurations and the data it stores using the Cloud Services.

## 3. ACCEPTABLE USE

3.1 Customer shall not use the Cloud Services to host or transmit unlawful content, to send unsolicited bulk messages, to mine cryptocurrency, or to interfere with the operation of any network or system.

3.2 Stratus may suspend a specific workload that is causing a security threat to the Cloud Services or to other customers, after notifying Customer and, where practicable, giving Customer an opportunity to remedy the issue.

3.3 Customer shall comply with the export control and sanctions laws of Singapore, India and any other country that apply to its use of the Cloud Services.

## 4. GOVERNANCE AND CHANGE CONTROL

4.1 Each Party shall nominate a relationship manager who shall be the primary point of contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Either Party may replace its relationship manager by written notice to the other.

4.2 The relationship managers shall meet monthly to review performance, open issues, risks and planned changes, and Stratus shall circulate minutes within five (5) Business Days of each meeting.

4.3 The Parties shall hold a quarterly business review attended by senior representatives of both Parties to review the overall relationship, performance trends, improvement initiatives and the forward plan.

4.4 Either Party may propose a change to the Cloud Services by submitting a Change Request. Stratus shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a Change Request from the Customer, provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on scope, timelines, resources and Fees.

4.5 No Change Request shall be binding unless it is signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. Until a Change Request is signed, the Parties shall continue to perform this Agreement without regard to the proposed change.

4.6 Stratus shall not be entitled to any additional Fees for work performed outside the agreed scope unless that work was the subject of a signed Change Request.

## SCHEDULE A — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

| Monthly availability | Service credit |
|---|---|
| < 99.95% and ≥ 99.5% | 10% of monthly Fees |
| < 99.5% and ≥ 99.0% | 25% of monthly Fees |
| < 99.0% | 50% of monthly Fees |

Measurement: Monthly Availability = (total minutes in the month − minutes during which the managed Kubernetes control plane API returns errors for all requests) ÷ total minutes in the month × 100, measured by Stratus's external probes at one-minute intervals.

Exclusions: scheduled maintenance announced at least five (5) Business Days in advance and performed between 01:00 and 05:00 IST; outages caused by Customer's applications or configurations; and Force Majeure Events.

Service Credits for any month shall not exceed fifty per cent (50%) of the monthly Fees for that month and are calculated on the committed monthly Fee in the Order Schedule.

## SCHEDULE B — SUPPORT SERVICES

| Severity | Description | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | Production cluster down or severely degraded | 15 minutes, 24x7 |
| Sev 2 | Significant impairment, workaround available | 1 hour, 24x7 |
| Sev 3 | Minor issue or question | 1 Business Day |

Support is available through Stratus portal, email (support@stratuscloud.sg) and a phone hotline. Customer's named technical contacts may open tickets; Stratus assigns a technical account manager who joins the monthly governance meeting.

## SCHEDULE C — INFORMATION SECURITY SCHEDULE

1. Governance: Stratus shall appoint a named information security officer and maintain documented security policies approved by its senior management and reviewed at least annually.

2. Access control: role-based access, unique user IDs, multi-factor authentication for all remote and privileged access, and automatic session time-out after fifteen (15) minutes of inactivity.

3. Encryption: Customer Data shall be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or an equivalent algorithm, with keys managed in accordance with Good Industry Practice.

4. Endpoint security: all devices used to access Customer Data shall run supported operating systems with current security patches, full-disk encryption and endpoint detection and response tooling.

5. Personnel security: background verification of all Personnel with access to Customer Data, covering identity, education, previous employment and criminal records to the extent permitted by Applicable Law.

6. Incident management: a documented incident response procedure, tested at least once a year, with escalation contacts shared with the Customer.
