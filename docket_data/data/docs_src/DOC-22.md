# SECURITY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Security Services Agreement is entered into at Bengaluru on 10 January 2025

BETWEEN

**SENTINEL GUARDING SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company licensed as a private security agency under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 (Karnataka licence No. PSARA/KA/2019/0418), having its registered office at No. 17, 1st Floor, Old Airport Road, Murugeshpalya, Bengaluru 560017 (the "**Agency**")

AND

**VELORA CONSUMER PRIVATE LIMITED**, CIN U74999KA2015PTC081234, having its registered office at Velora House, No. 14, Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025 (the "**Company**").

## 1. SERVICES

1.1 The Agency shall provide trained and PSARA-compliant security guards and supervisors at the Company's premises at Residency Road and Whitefield, Bengaluru, in accordance with the deployment plan in Schedule 2.

## 2. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

2.1 The Company shall pay a monthly charge of ₹1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only), exclusive of GST, for the deployment in Schedule 2 (₹18,00,000 per annum).

2.2 Invoices shall be raised monthly and paid within forty-five (45) days of receipt, subject to submission of wage, PF and ESI challans for the previous month.

2.3 Charges for any extended or continued period shall be increased by four per cent (4%).

## 3. TERM

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on 16 January 2025 and remain in force until 15 January 2027 (the "**Term**").

3.2 Upon expiry of the Term, this Agreement may be extended for a further period of twelve (12) months on such terms as the parties may mutually agree in writing.

3.3 Unless either party notifies the other in writing, not less than forty-five (45) days prior to the expiry of the Term, of its intention not to continue, the Services shall continue to be provided on the terms of this Agreement.

## 4. SERVICE STANDARDS AND PENALTIES

4.1 For each guard-shift not covered as per Schedule 2, the Company shall deduct the pro-rata charge for that shift plus a penalty of ₹1,000.

4.2 Any guard found sleeping or absent from post shall be replaced within four (4) hours, failing which a penalty of ₹2,000 per incident shall apply.

## 5. TERMINATION

5.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the Agency.

5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice.

## 6. NOTICES

6.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by email or by courier to the addresses of the parties stated above, or to such other address as a party may notify.

## 7. GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
