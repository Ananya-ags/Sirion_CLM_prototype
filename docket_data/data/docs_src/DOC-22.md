## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Agency shall provide trained and PSARA-compliant security guards and supervisors at the Company's premises at Residency Road and Whitefield, Bengaluru, in accordance with the deployment plan in Schedule 2.

2.2 The Agency shall operate a 24x7 control room that the Supervisor and Guards can reach by telephone, and shall carry out at least one surprise night check at each of the Premises every week.

2.3 The Guards shall control access at each post, maintain visitor, material and vehicle registers, carry out patrols at the intervals stated in the Post Orders, and report any incident to the Company's Admin team without delay.

2.4 The Company may change the deployment plan by giving the Agency at least seven (7) days' written notice, and the Charges shall be adjusted pro rata to the number of Shifts in the revised plan.

## 3. LICENSING, VETTING AND TRAINING

3.1 The Agency shall hold a valid licence under PSARA for the State of Karnataka throughout the period of this Agreement, and shall give the Company a copy of each renewed licence within seven (7) days of its issue.

3.2 The Agency shall deploy only Guards who are at least eighteen (18) years old, are medically fit, have completed police verification of their antecedents, and have completed the training prescribed under PSARA, including at least one hundred (100) hours of classroom instruction and sixty (60) hours of field training.

3.3 Each Guard shall wear the Agency's uniform and display a photo identity card issued by the Agency at all times while on duty.

3.4 The Agency shall maintain a file for each Guard containing proof of identity, address, police verification, training and medical fitness, and shall make it available for inspection by the Company on request.

## 4. POST ORDERS AND CONDUCT

4.1 The Guards shall follow the Post Orders and any lawful instruction of the Company's Admin Manager on matters of site security.

4.2 No Guard shall consume alcohol or intoxicants on duty, use a mobile phone at a post except for duty purposes, or leave a post before being relieved.

4.3 The Agency shall rotate Guards between posts and between the Premises at least once every six (6) months, and shall not withdraw the Supervisor without giving the Company forty-five (45) days' written notice and proposing a replacement acceptable to the Company.

4.4 The Agency shall report any theft, intrusion, fire, injury or other serious incident to the Company's Admin Manager by telephone immediately and in writing within twenty-four (24) hours.

## 5. CHARGES AND PAYMENT

5.1 The Company shall pay a monthly charge of ₹1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only), exclusive of GST, for the deployment in Schedule 2 (₹18,00,000 per annum).

5.2 Invoices shall be raised monthly and paid within forty-five (45) days of receipt, subject to submission of wage, PF and ESI challans for the previous month.

5.3 Charges for any extended or continued period shall be increased by four per cent (4%).

5.4 The monthly charge is inclusive of wages, statutory contributions, bonus, uniforms, equipment, supervision and the Agency's service charge, and is set out in more detail in Schedule 1.

## 6. LABOUR LAW COMPLIANCE

6.1 The Agency shall be the employer of all Guards and Supervisors and shall comply with all labour laws applicable to them, including the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970, the Code on Wages, 2019, the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 and the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948.

6.2 The Agency shall pay wages to its Guards by bank transfer on or before the seventh (7th) day of each month, at not less than the minimum wages notified for security guards in Karnataka.

6.3 The Agency shall submit with each invoice copies of the wage register, attendance register and the provident fund and ESI challans for the previous month, and the Company may withhold payment of an invoice until they are received.

6.4 The Agency shall indemnify the Company against any claim made against the Company as principal employer arising from the Agency's failure to comply with this Clause.

## 7. GOVERNANCE AND CHANGE CONTROL

7.1 Each Party shall nominate a relationship manager who shall be the primary point of contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Either Party may replace its relationship manager by written notice to the other.

7.2 The relationship managers shall meet monthly to review performance, open issues, risks and planned changes, and the Agency shall circulate minutes within five (5) Business Days of each meeting.

7.3 The Parties shall hold a quarterly business review attended by senior representatives of both Parties to review the overall relationship, performance trends, improvement initiatives and the forward plan.

7.4 Either Party may propose a change to the Services by submitting a Change Request. The Agency shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a Change Request from the Company, provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on scope, timelines, resources and Charges.

7.5 No Change Request shall be binding unless it is signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. Until a Change Request is signed, the Parties shall continue to perform this Agreement without regard to the proposed change.

7.6 The Agency shall not be entitled to any additional Charges for work performed outside the agreed scope unless that work was the subject of a signed Change Request.

## 9. DATA PROTECTION

9.1 The Parties acknowledge that, in respect of Personal Data processed under this Agreement, the Company is the Data Fiduciary and the Agency is a Data Processor acting on behalf of the Company.

9.2 The Agency shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Company, only for the purposes of this Agreement and in compliance with Data Protection Law. The Agency shall promptly inform the Company if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

9.3 The Agency shall implement and maintain reasonable security safeguards, including the measures agreed between the Parties in writing, to protect Personal Data against personal data breach, as required by Section 8(5) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9.4 The Agency shall notify the Company of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it, and shall provide all information and assistance the Company reasonably requires to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals.

9.5 The Agency shall assist the Company in responding to requests from Data Principals to exercise their rights under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including rights of access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal.

9.6 The Agency shall not transfer Personal Data outside India except to a country or territory that has not been restricted by notification of the Central Government under Section 16 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and only with the Company's prior written consent.

9.7 The Agency shall ensure that its Personnel who have access to Personal Data are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and receive training in data protection at least once a year.

9.8 On completion of the purpose for which Personal Data was provided, or on the Company's request, the Agency shall erase that Personal Data and certify the erasure in writing, save where retention is required by Applicable Law.

## 13. TERM

13.1 This Agreement shall commence on 16 January 2025 and remain in force until 15 January 2027 (the "**Term**").

13.3 Unless either party notifies the other in writing, not less than forty-five (45) days prior to the expiry of the Term, of its intention not to continue, the Services shall continue to be provided on the terms of this Agreement.

## 14. SERVICE STANDARDS AND PENALTIES

14.1 For each guard-shift not covered as per Schedule 2, the Company shall deduct the pro-rata charge for that shift plus a penalty of ₹1,000.

14.2 Any guard found sleeping or absent from post shall be replaced within four (4) hours, failing which a penalty of ₹2,000 per incident shall apply.

14.3 The Company's Admin team shall audit the attendance registers each month and share a shift-coverage report with the Agency by the seventh (7th) day of the following month. Deductions under Clause 14.1 shall be made from the invoice for the month to which the report relates.

## 15. TERMINATION

15.3 The Company may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the Agency's licence under PSARA is suspended or cancelled.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 A "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party, including acts of God, flood, earthquake, epidemic or pandemic, war, terrorism, riot, civil commotion, strikes (other than strikes of the affected Party's own Personnel), lockouts, fire, explosion, and acts of any Governmental Authority, but excluding lack of funds.

16.2 Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations to the extent caused by a Force Majeure Event, provided that the affected Party: (a) notifies the other Party promptly of the nature and expected duration of the Force Majeure Event; and (b) uses all reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effect.

16.3 The Agency shall maintain and test, at least once a year, a business continuity and disaster recovery plan, and a Force Majeure Event shall not excuse the Agency from performing that plan.

16.4 If a Force Majeure Event prevents the performance of all or a material part of the Services for a continuous period of more than forty-five (45) days, the unaffected Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice.
