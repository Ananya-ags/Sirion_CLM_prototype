## 2. ENGAGEMENT STRUCTURE

2.1 The Supplier shall provide qualified technology professionals ("Resources") to the Client under one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement. Each SOW shall form part of and be governed by this Agreement.

3.2 The Supplier shall complete onboarding formalities, including execution by each Resource of the Client's acceptable-use and confidentiality undertakings, within fifteen (15) days of the Resource's start date.

3.3 The Supplier shall ensure that each Resource completes the Client's mandatory information security and prevention of sexual harassment training within the first month of deployment.

5.3 The Parties shall hold a quarterly business review attended by senior representatives of both Parties to review the overall relationship, performance trends, improvement initiatives and the forward plan.

5.4 Either Party may propose a change to the Services by submitting a Change Request. The Supplier shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a Change Request from the Client, provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on scope, timelines, resources and fees.

5.5 No Change Request shall be binding unless it is signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. Until a Change Request is signed, the Parties shall continue to perform this Agreement without regard to the proposed change.

5.6 The Supplier shall not be entitled to any additional fees for work performed outside the agreed scope unless that work was the subject of a signed Change Request.
