## 2. APPOINTMENT

2.1 The Client appoints the Agency as its non-exclusive digital and social media agency for the Client's consumer brands in India.

2.2 The Client may appoint other agencies for any services, including services similar to the Services, and the Agency shall cooperate with them in good faith.

## 3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

3.1 The Agency shall provide the Deliverables each month in accordance with the content calendar agreed with the Client by the 25th day of the preceding month.

3.2 The Client may change the Deliverables for a month by giving the Agency thirty (30) days' written notice, provided that the total effort does not materially exceed the effort assumed in Schedule 2.

3.3 The Agency shall manage paid media only in accordance with an approved Media Plan and shall not commit the Client to any media spend above the approved Media Plan.

## 4. APPROVALS AND CLIENT MATERIALS

4.1 No creative, post or campaign shall be published without the prior written approval of the Client's brand manager in accordance with the approval matrix in Schedule 3.

4.2 The Client shall provide brand guidelines, product information, claims substantiation and other materials reasonably required by the Agency, and warrants that it has the right to provide them.

## 5. INFLUENCER MANAGEMENT

5.1 The Agency shall identify, vet and propose influencers for the Client's approval, sharing for each a profile covering audience demographics, engagement rate, past brand associations and any history of controversy or regulatory action.

5.2 Each influencer shall be engaged under a written agreement approved by the Client that requires compliance with the ASCI influencer guidelines, prohibits undisclosed paid endorsements and grants the Client the right to reuse the content on its own channels for at least twelve (12) months.

5.3 The Agency shall monitor influencer posts within twenty-four (24) hours of publication and shall arrange the immediate removal of any post that has not been approved or that breaches Applicable Law or the Client's brand guidelines.

5.4 The Agency shall not pay any influencer in cash, and all payments to influencers shall be made through banking channels with tax deducted at source as required under Section 194R or Section 194J of the Income-tax Act, 1961, as applicable.

## 6. MEDIA BUYING, REBATES AND PLATFORM ACCOUNTS

6.1 All advertising accounts on Meta, Google, YouTube and other platforms used for the Client shall be opened in the name of the Client, with the Agency added as a partner or user. The Client shall retain administrator rights at all times.

6.2 The Agency shall pass on to the Client, in full, all volume rebates, credits, incentives and bonuses received from any publisher or platform in respect of the Client's media spends, and shall disclose them in the monthly performance report.

6.3 The Agency shall not charge any mark-up or commission on media spends other than as expressly stated in this Agreement or an approved Media Plan.

6.4 The Client may, on reasonable notice, inspect the platform dashboards and billing statements relating to its media spends to verify the amounts invoiced by the Agency.

## 7. AGENCY PERSONNEL

7.1 The Agency shall assign a dedicated team to the Client's account, including the Account Lead and a Creative Director, as set out in Schedule 2.

7.2 The Agency shall give the Client at least sixty (60) days' written notice before replacing the Account Lead or the Creative Director assigned to the Client, save where the person leaves the Agency's employment.

## 8. GOVERNANCE AND CHANGE CONTROL

8.1 Each Party shall nominate a relationship manager who shall be the primary point of contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Either Party may replace its relationship manager by written notice to the other.

8.2 The relationship managers shall meet at least once a month to review performance, open issues, risks and planned changes, and the Agency shall circulate minutes within five (5) Business Days of each meeting.

8.3 The Parties shall hold a quarterly business review attended by senior representatives of both Parties to review the overall relationship, performance trends, improvement initiatives and the forward plan.

8.4 Either Party may propose a change to the Services by submitting a Change Request. The Agency shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a Change Request from the Client, provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on scope, timelines, resources and Retainer Fee.

8.5 No Change Request shall be binding unless it is signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. Until a Change Request is signed, the Parties shall continue to perform this Agreement without regard to the proposed change.

8.6 The Agency shall not be entitled to any additional Retainer Fee for work performed outside the agreed scope unless that work was the subject of a signed Change Request.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Each Party retains all right, title and interest in and to its Background IP. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of any Background IP from one Party to the other.

9.2 The Client grants the Agency a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free licence for the duration of this Agreement to use the Client's Background IP and Customer Data solely to the extent necessary to perform the Services.

9.3 Save as expressly provided otherwise, all Intellectual Property Rights in materials created specifically for the Client by the Agency or its Personnel under this Agreement shall vest in the Client on creation or, if vesting on creation is not possible, on payment of the Retainer Fee relating to them, and the Agency hereby assigns such rights to the Client with full title guarantee.

9.4 To the extent any of the Agency's Background IP is embedded in materials delivered to the Client, the Agency grants the Client and its Affiliates a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, copy and modify that Background IP as part of those materials.

9.5 The Agency shall procure that its Personnel waive, to the extent permitted by law, all moral rights in materials created for the Client, including the rights conferred by Section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957.

9.6 Neither Party shall use the other Party's name, logo or trade marks in any publicity, marketing material or client list without the other Party's prior written consent.

## 10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 The Recipient shall: (a) keep the Discloser's Confidential Information confidential; (b) use it only for the purpose of performing or receiving the benefit of this Agreement; and (c) not disclose it to any person except as permitted by this Clause 10.

## 11. DATA PROTECTION

11.1 The Parties acknowledge that, in respect of Personal Data processed under this Agreement, the Client is the Data Fiduciary and the Agency is a Data Processor acting on behalf of the Client.

11.2 The Agency shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Client, only for the purposes of this Agreement and in compliance with Data Protection Law. The Agency shall promptly inform the Client if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

11.3 The Agency shall implement and maintain reasonable security safeguards, including the measures described in the Information Security Schedule, to protect Personal Data against personal data breach, as required by Section 8(5) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

11.4 The Agency shall notify the Client of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it, and shall provide all information and assistance the Client reasonably requires to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals.

11.5 The Agency shall assist the Client in responding to requests from Data Principals to exercise their rights under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including rights of access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal.

11.6 The Agency shall not transfer Personal Data outside India except to a country or territory that has not been restricted by notification of the Central Government under Section 16 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and only with the Client's prior written consent.

11.7 The Agency shall ensure that its Personnel who have access to Personal Data are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and receive training in data protection at least once a year.

11.8 On completion of the purpose for which Personal Data was provided, or on the Client's request, the Agency shall erase that Personal Data and certify the erasure in writing, save where retention is required by Applicable Law.
