## 1. SERVICES

1.1 TalkWave shall provide the connectivity services listed in Annexure 1: two (2) Internet Leased Lines of 1 Gbps each and MPLS VPN links connecting the Customer's Residency Road office, Whitefield office and Hoskote warehouse (the "**Services**").

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on 1 March 2025 and shall continue for an initial period of twenty-four (24) months ending on 28 February 2027 (the "**Initial Period**").

2.2 This Agreement shall thereafter renew automatically for successive periods of twenty-four (24) months each unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal no less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the Initial Period or the then-current renewal period.

## 3. CHARGES

3.1 Monthly Recurring Charges ("**MRC**"): ₹1,10,000 (Rupees One Lakh Ten Thousand only) per month, exclusive of GST, for all Services in Annexure 1.

3.2 One-time installation charges have been waived.

3.3 On each renewal, the MRC may be revised in line with the year-on-year change in the All-India Consumer Price Index (Combined), subject to a maximum increase of four per cent (4%).

## 4. BILLING AND PAYMENT

4.1 TalkWave shall invoice the MRC monthly in advance on the first day of each month.

4.2 Payment shall be due within forty-five (45) days from the invoice date.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 TalkWave guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for each ILL circuit and a mean time to restore of four (4) hours.

5.2 Service credits for any shortfall shall be calculated and credited in accordance with Annexure 2.

## 6. TERMINATION

6.1 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving sixty (60) days' written notice, subject to payment of an early termination charge equal to two (2) months' MRC if termination takes effect during the Initial Period.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that remains unremedied for thirty (30) days after written notice.

## 7. NOTICES

7.1 All notices shall be in writing and sent by email to TalkWave at contracts@talkwave.in and to the Customer at legal@velora.in.

## 8. GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru.
