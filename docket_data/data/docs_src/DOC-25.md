## 2. SERVICES

2.1 TalkWave shall provide the connectivity services listed in Annexure 1: two (2) Internet Leased Lines of 1 Gbps each and MPLS VPN links connecting the Customer's Residency Road office, Whitefield office and Hoskote warehouse (the "**Services**").

2.2 TalkWave shall provide a block of eight (8) static public IPv4 addresses on each ILL and shall support IPv6 on request.

2.3 TalkWave shall provide a 24x7 network operations centre and a named key account manager, and shall share the escalation matrix with the Customer's IT team.

## 4. PROVISIONING, ACCEPTANCE AND CHANGES

4.1 TalkWave shall commission each Circuit and share a test report. The Customer shall accept the Circuit, or notify any defects, within five (5) Business Days of receiving the test report, failing which the Circuit shall be deemed accepted.

4.2 Billing for each Circuit starts on the date of acceptance or deemed acceptance.

4.3 The Customer may request a bandwidth upgrade at any time; TalkWave shall implement it within fifteen (15) Business Days at the rates in its then-current enterprise price list.

4.4 Requests to shift a Circuit to a new address must be given at least ninety (90) days in advance and are subject to feasibility and one-time shifting charges.

5.2 The relationship managers shall meet at least once a month to review performance, open issues, risks and planned changes, and TalkWave shall circulate minutes within five (5) Business Days of each meeting.

5.3 The Parties shall hold a quarterly business review attended by senior representatives of both Parties to review the overall relationship, performance trends, improvement initiatives and the forward plan.

5.4 Either Party may propose a change to the Services by submitting a Change Request. TalkWave shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a Change Request from the Customer, provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on scope, timelines, resources and Charges.

5.5 No Change Request shall be binding unless it is signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. Until a Change Request is signed, the Parties shall continue to perform this Agreement without regard to the proposed change.

5.6 TalkWave shall not be entitled to any additional Charges for work performed outside the agreed scope unless that work was the subject of a signed Change Request.
