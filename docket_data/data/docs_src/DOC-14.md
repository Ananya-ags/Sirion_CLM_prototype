## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Provider shall provide dedicated last-mile and inter-city delivery services, including a dedicated fleet of fourteen (14) light commercial vehicles with drivers, for Customer's e-commerce and marketing collateral shipments within Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Telangana (the "**Services**").

2.2 The Services are described in further detail in Annexure A (Service Description) and priced in Annexure B (Rates and Fuel Index).

2.3 The Provider shall ensure that every vehicle in the Dedicated Fleet is not more than five (5) years old, is fitted with a working GPS tracker visible to the Customer's control tower, and carries a valid fitness certificate, permit and insurance.

2.4 The Provider shall keep at least two (2) spare vehicles available at its Hoskote hub to replace any vehicle in the Dedicated Fleet that breaks down, within four (4) hours of a breakdown being reported.

## 3. OPERATIONS AND PROOF OF DELIVERY

3.1 The Provider shall collect Consignments from the Customer's Hoskote warehouse between 07:00 and 11:00 and between 15:00 and 18:00 each day, and shall scan every Consignment into its system at pick-up.

3.2 The Provider shall upload a Proof of Delivery for every delivered Consignment within twenty-four (24) hours of delivery.

3.3 The Provider shall attempt delivery at least twice before marking a Consignment for Return to Origin, and shall return each RTO Consignment to the Customer's warehouse within seven (7) days of the final attempt.

3.4 The Customer shall notify the Provider of any claim for loss of or damage to a Consignment within forty-five (45) days of the scheduled delivery date, failing which the claim shall be deemed waived.

3.5 The Provider shall not open, repack or relabel any Consignment except where required by a Governmental Authority, and shall record any such inspection on the manifest.

## 4. DRIVERS AND PERSONNEL

4.1 The Provider shall ensure that every driver holds a valid driving licence for the relevant vehicle class under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, has completed police verification, and has received training in safe driving and courteous customer handling.

4.2 The Provider shall be solely responsible for the wages, overtime, provident fund, employees' state insurance and other statutory dues of its drivers and other Personnel.

4.3 No driver shall drive for more than ten (10) hours in any day, and the Provider shall maintain duty logs that the Customer may inspect on request.

4.4 The Provider shall enforce a zero-tolerance policy on alcohol and drugs, and shall immediately remove from the Services any driver found in breach of it.

## 5. GOVERNANCE AND CHANGE CONTROL

5.1 Each Party shall nominate a relationship manager who shall be the primary point of contact for day-to-day matters under this Agreement. Either Party may replace its relationship manager by written notice to the other.

5.2 The relationship managers shall meet at least once a month to review performance, open issues, risks and planned changes, and the Provider shall circulate minutes within five (5) Business Days of each meeting.

5.3 The Parties shall hold a quarterly business review attended by senior representatives of both Parties to review the overall relationship, performance trends, improvement initiatives and the forward plan.

5.4 Either Party may propose a change to the Services by submitting a Change Request. The Provider shall, within ten (10) Business Days of receiving a Change Request from the Customer, provide a written impact assessment describing the effect of the change on scope, timelines, resources and Charges.

5.5 No Change Request shall be binding unless it is signed by authorised representatives of both Parties. Until a Change Request is signed, the Parties shall continue to perform this Agreement without regard to the proposed change.

5.6 The Provider shall not be entitled to any additional Charges for work performed outside the agreed scope unless that work was the subject of a signed Change Request.

## 13. CHARGES

## 15. SERVICE LEVELS

15.1 The Provider shall achieve on-time delivery of at least 97% of consignments in each month.

15.2 For each full percentage point by which monthly on-time delivery falls below 97%, the Customer shall receive a credit of two per cent (2%) of that month's fixed charges, up to a maximum of fifteen per cent (15%).

15.3 The Provider shall send the Customer a monthly service report by the seventh (7th) Business Day of the following month, showing On-Time Delivery by lane, RTO rates, POD compliance and claims, and shall apply any credit due under Clause 15.2 on its next invoice.
