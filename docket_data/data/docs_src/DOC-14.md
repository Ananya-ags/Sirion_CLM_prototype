# LOGISTICS SERVICES MASTER AGREEMENT

This Logistics Services Master Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is dated 11 March 2025 and is made between:

(1) **SWIFTMOVE LOGISTICS PRIVATE LIMITED**, CIN U63030KA2017PTC101558, GSTIN 29AAHCS1558Q1ZJ, whose registered office is at Plot No. 41, KIADB Industrial Area, Hoskote, Bengaluru Rural 562114 (the "**Provider**"); and

(2) **VELORA CONSUMER PRIVATE LIMITED**, CIN U74999KA2015PTC081234, whose registered office is at Velora House, No. 14, Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025 (the "**Customer**").

## 1. SERVICES

1.1 The Provider shall provide dedicated last-mile and inter-city delivery services, including a dedicated fleet of fourteen (14) light commercial vehicles with drivers, for Customer's e-commerce and marketing collateral shipments within Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Telangana (the "**Services**").

1.2 The Services are described in further detail in Annexure A (Service Description) and priced in Annexure B (Rates and Fuel Index).

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 This Agreement takes effect on 16 March 2025 and, unless terminated earlier, continues until 15 March 2027 (the "**Initial Term**").

2.2 On each expiry, this Agreement shall renew automatically for a further period of one (1) year unless either Party serves written notice of non-renewal on the other at least forty-five (45) days before the date of expiry.

## 3. CHARGES

3.1 The Customer shall pay the monthly fixed charges and variable charges set out in Annexure B. The estimated annual value of this Agreement is ₹41,00,000, exclusive of GST.

3.2 The fixed charges shall be adjusted each month for movements in the diesel price in accordance with the fuel index mechanism in Annexure B. No other price escalation shall apply during the Initial Term or any renewal.

## 4. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Provider shall invoice monthly, with a delivery manifest summary attached.

4.2 Payment shall be made within forty-five (45) days from the date of receipt of a valid GST-compliant invoice.

## 5. SERVICE LEVELS

5.1 The Provider shall achieve on-time delivery of at least 97% of consignments in each month.

5.2 For each full percentage point by which monthly on-time delivery falls below 97%, the Customer shall receive a credit of two per cent (2%) of that month's fixed charges, up to a maximum of fifteen per cent (15%).

## 6. LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

6.1 The Provider shall maintain goods-in-transit insurance for not less than ₹25,00,000 per vehicle per trip.

## 7. TERMINATION

7.1 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving sixty (60) days' written notice.

7.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.

## 8. NOTICES

8.1 Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by email to: Provider — legal@swiftmove.in; Customer — legal@velora.in.

## 9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru.
