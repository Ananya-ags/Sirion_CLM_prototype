Extract from: Field Services Contract — Ravensworth Engineering Services LLP

Schedule C, para 4. The Contract stands extended for a further twelve months at the end of each contract year unless the Purchaser informs the Contractor, no later than forty (40) Working Days before the end of that contract year, that it does not wish the Contract to continue. For this purpose "Working Day" excludes Saturdays, Sundays and gazetted public holidays in the State of Karnataka.
