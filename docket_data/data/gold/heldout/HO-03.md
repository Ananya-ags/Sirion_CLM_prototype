17.2 Price review. With effect from the first day of every renewal period, the licence fee shall go up by whichever is higher of (i) six per cent (6%) and (ii) the annual change in the Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (CPI-IW) released by the Labour Bureau, Shimla; provided that the total increase applied at any single renewal shall in no case exceed ten per cent (10%).
