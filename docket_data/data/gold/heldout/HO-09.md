Section 6.4 Rebates. For every 0.1% (or part thereof) by which power availability in a month is below 99.98%, the Provider shall rebate five per cent (5%) of the monthly rack charges for the affected racks, subject to an overall monthly cap of fifty per cent (50%). Rebates are adjusted against the following month's bill.
