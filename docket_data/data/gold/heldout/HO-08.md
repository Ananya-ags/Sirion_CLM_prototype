Extract from: Payroll Outsourcing Agreement — Tallymark Payroll Services Pvt Ltd

19.1 This Agreement may be brought to an end before the expiry of the Term only by a party, by written notice, where the other party (a) is in material default and fails to put it right within twenty-one (21) days, or (b) is admitted into a corporate insolvency resolution process. No other ground of early termination shall be available to either party.
